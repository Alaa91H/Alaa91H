## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Alaa**, an Android & systems developer who enjoys building things end-to-end — from native Kotlin apps and automation engines to custom kernels, ROMs, and the CI pipelines that ship them.

I'm currently focused on the **Nexa** family of Android projects (an automation engine, a companion SDK, and more), maintaining kernels for devices like the **POCO F5** and **Redmi Note 5 Pro**, and crafting practical tools such as the **NOVA Download Manager** and Islamic community services.
